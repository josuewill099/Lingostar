## **The Power of Historical Interaction in Education**

Why does **historical interaction** matter? Research shows that active engagement—asking questions and receiving immediate feedback—leads to significantly higher retention rates than passive reading.

By using **PeopleAI**, students can:

1.  **Develop Critical Thinking:** Compare the viewpoints of different historical figures on the same event.
2.  **Enhance Empathy:** Understand the personal motivations and pressures faced by leaders in the past.
3.  **Synthesize Complex Ideas:** Have an **AI history tutor** break down difficult concepts like the Theory of Relativity or the Magna Carta in plain English.
